# Studio ↔ employees — junior development arc

The studio commits to its juniors on development, not on tenure (outline-charter-2024 §13). Every junior has a named senior counterpart and a quarterly development plan with the operations lead. Time spent on development is billable to the studio, not to clients (outline-charter-2024 §10).

The current state: one junior designer hired March 2025, paired with a senior, two studio-internal artefacts shipped in her first nine months (the studio website redesign, the internal proposal template), now shadowing her second client engagement. The studio plans to bring on one junior strategist and one junior product designer in 2026 (outline-annual-review-2025).
