# Studio ↔ vendor partners — 30-day payment

The studio pays vendor partner invoices within 30 days of receipt regardless of whether the underlying client has paid (outline-charter-2024 §18). This is the structural commitment that lets the studio work with the small studios and freelance specialists it wants to work with — production-only work that stays in vendor relationships rather than getting absorbed in-house (outline-charter-2024 §11).

The two-week vendor-invoice cycle run by [operations](../nodes/units/operations.md) operators is what enables the commitment. In 2025 every invoice was paid on time (outline-annual-review-2025).
