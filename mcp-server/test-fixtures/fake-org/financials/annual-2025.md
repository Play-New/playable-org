# Annual finance summary, 2025

## Revenue

€4.6M total, up from €3.4M in 2024 (outline-annual-review-2025).

| Source | Amount | % | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-market engagements | €2.8M | 61% | 14 engagements, ~€200k avg |
| Enterprise engagements | €1.5M | 33% | 2 engagements (€600k + €900k) |
| Studio-internal investment | €0.3M | 6% | unallocated to client work |

## Operating costs by area

| Area | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Strategy](../nodes/units/strategy.md) | €0.6M | salaries, no other significant costs |
| [Design](../nodes/units/design.md) | €0.9M | salaries plus type-foundry license bench |

| **Total** | **€3.5M** | |

Operating margin: €1.1M (24%) (outline-annual-review-2025).

## Vendor partner spend

€0.6M total, all paid within 30 days per the [vendor payment commitment](../commitments/studio-vendors-payments.md):

- Web development partners: €0.3M
- Illustrators and photographers: €0.15M
- Type-foundry licensing: €0.05M
- Printers: €0.07M
- Other: €0.03M

## Headcount

23 people end-of-year, up from 19 in 2024. Six people short of the 30-person cap declared in the charter (outline-charter-2024 §20).

| Area | People |
|---|---|
| Strategy | 5 |
| Design | 7 |
| Digital product | 5 |
| Client services | 3 |
| Operations | 3 |

## Pitch conversion

11 pitches entered in 2025, 8 won (73% conversion). Three lost: one for timeline (would have broken the senior-eyes rule), one for media-buying scope (charter §6), one because brand and product engineering were locked at separate vendors (charter §1) (outline-annual-review-2025).
