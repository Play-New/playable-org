# Brand book

The design lead builds the brand book personally on every engagement. The brand book is the artefact the studio is best known for and the design lead does not delegate it (outline-roles-2025).

Mid-level designers may produce individual brand-book chapters under the lead's review, but the integration into a single artefact is the lead's hand (outline-roles-2025).
