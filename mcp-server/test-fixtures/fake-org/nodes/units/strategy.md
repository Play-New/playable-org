# Strategy

The strategy area runs every brand-strategy engagement end-to-end. Positioning, audience research, naming systems, narrative workshops, competitive audits — the area produces a written brief that the design area builds on top of (outline-charter-2024 §1).

The area carries five people: the strategy lead and four mid-level strategists, no juniors at this date (outline-roles-2025). The lead scopes engagements at pitch, facilitates the narrative workshop on-site with the client (typically two half-days), writes the positioning document, runs audience research with one strategist, and hands a written brief to the design lead. Strategists run audience research interviews, transcribe and synthesise, write competitive audits, and develop naming systems on briefed projects (outline-roles-2025).

## Cross-area handovers

The strategy area produces the brief that the [design](design.md) area builds the identity system on top of. Strategy also feeds the [digital product](digital-product.md) area when product engagements include a positioning component. Client services runs the kickoff workshop where strategy meets the client for the first time (outline-charter-2024 §1, §4).
