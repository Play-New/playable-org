# Digital product

The digital product area runs UI design for B2B web and apps, builds design systems for clients who maintain their own product engineering, and embeds UX research with the client's product team when the engagement asks for it (outline-charter-2024 §3).

The area carries five people: the product lead, two senior product designers, two mid-level product designers (outline-roles-2025). The product lead has stronger engineering literacy than the design lead — they can read and review front-end code from client teams and translate design decisions into engineering tickets. Senior product designers own UI design for individual engagements (prototyping, accessibility audits, pairing with client front-end developers). Mid-level product designers support seniors and own design-system documentation when the client is building a system the studio will hand off (outline-roles-2025).

## Cross-area handovers

Digital product builds on top of [strategy](strategy.md) (positioning) and [design](design.md) (identity system). On enterprise engagements digital product also runs design-system handover work in tandem with the client's product team — this is the embedded-presence pattern documented for enterprise relationships (outline-charter-2024 §16).
