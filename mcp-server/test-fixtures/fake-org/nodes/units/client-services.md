# Client services

Client services runs the kickoff workshop for every engagement (scoping, calendar, weekly cadence, single point of contact on the client side), runs the weekly check-in calls, manages the asset handover ritual at the end of each engagement, and runs scoping calls for new pitches alongside the relevant discipline lead (outline-charter-2024 §4, outline-roles-2025).

The area carries three people: the lead and two account/project managers. Account managers run the weekly check-ins on assigned engagements (typically three each), manage asset handover logistics, and chase client-side approvals. The lead sits at every kickoff and pitch (outline-roles-2025).

## Cross-area handovers

Client services touches every engagement and every discipline. The lead is in the room when [strategy](strategy.md), [design](design.md), or [digital product](digital-product.md) hand to the client; account managers are the day-to-day point of contact for clients on assigned engagements. Operations supports client services on contracts, invoicing, and vendor coordination (outline-charter-2024 §4, §5).
