# Mid-market clients

The bread-and-butter of the studio. Mid-market companies that engage Outline for the strategy → identity → digital product flow on fixed-price scopes agreed at pitch (outline-charter-2024 §15).

In 2025 the studio ran fourteen mid-market engagements totalling €2.8M (61% of revenue), averaging €200k each. Eleven closed on time and on fixed price; three required scope-renegotiation conversations handled by the founder (outline-annual-review-2025).
