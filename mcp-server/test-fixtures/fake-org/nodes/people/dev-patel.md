# Dev Patel

Digital product lead. Dev runs UI design, UX research, and design-system construction for product engagements. He has stronger engineering literacy than [Lena Thorvaldsen](lena-thorvaldsen.md) — Dev can read and review front-end code from client teams and translate design decisions into engineering tickets (outline-roles-2025).

The product area under Dev has two senior product designers (each owning one engagement at a time) and two mid-level product designers (supporting seniors and owning design-system documentation when the client is building a system the studio will hand off). On the studio's enterprise engagements Dev or one of the seniors typically embeds with the client product team for the duration (outline-roles-2025, outline-charter-2024 §16).
