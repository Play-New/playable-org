# Studio ↔ enterprise clients — baseline engagement

The studio's smaller-volume, higher-stakes commitment. One or two enterprise relationships per year, 6-9 months, embedded presence, higher pricing (outline-charter-2024 §16). The cap of one or two is a design choice — past that, enterprise gravity pulls the studio toward agency-of-record bloat (outline-charter-2024 §1, §20).

The 2025 industrial-machinery engagement opened a new pattern: 12-month follow-on retainer with one embedded product designer two days per week. The operations lead is tracking this as either an evolving commitment shape or as scope creep that needs renegotiation (outline-annual-review-2025).
