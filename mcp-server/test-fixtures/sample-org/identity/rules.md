# Rules

Six governance principles, named in the studio charter.

**Senior eyes on every output.** No client deliverable leaves the studio without the lead of its discipline reviewing it. The creative director (founder) reviews any deliverable that combines two or more disciplines (outline-charter-2024 §9).

**Junior development is structural, not opportunistic.** Every junior designer or strategist has a named senior counterpart and a quarterly development plan. Time spent on development is billable to the studio, not to clients (outline-charter-2024 §10).

**Vendor partners over employees for production-only work.** Web development, video editing, illustration, photography, type-foundry licensing — these stay vendor relationships. The studio does not try to be a production shop (outline-charter-2024 §11).

**Prices are clear and posted.** Engagement scopes carry a fixed price disclosed at pitch. The studio does not bill hourly; it bills deliverables. Disputes about price changes go to the founder (outline-charter-2024 §12).

**The studio commits to its employees on development, not on tenure.** Junior employees can expect a structured development arc; the studio does not guarantee permanent employment if the work doesn't fit (outline-charter-2024 §13).

**Direction decisions are consensus-based among the leads.** New disciplines, new client tiers, new offices need consensus across the three discipline leads + the founder. Hiring within an existing discipline is the lead's call (outline-charter-2024 §14).
