# Mission

Outline & Co. helps mid-market companies articulate what they sell, design how it shows up, and ship the digital products that carry it forward. The studio works as a single team across three disciplines — strategy, design, and digital product — held together by tight client services and a small operations function (outline-charter-2024 §1).

The studio takes on engagements where the brand and the product are co-developed rather than where one is asked to dress the other after the fact. This is what distinguishes Outline from a logo-only design studio and from a product-only digital agency (outline-charter-2024 §1).
