# Limits

Three things the studio explicitly does not do.

**Paid-media campaigns.** Outline does not buy or place advertising; clients work with media agencies for that (outline-charter-2024 §6).

**Logos as one-off deliverables.** A logo without an identity system around it goes stale within twelve months and the studio refuses to ship work it knows will go stale. Identity work is sold as a system, not as a single mark (outline-charter-2024 §7).

**Subcontracting client work to other agencies under the studio's name.** If a client need exceeds Outline's capacity the studio says so and recommends another studio in its network. The studio does not present another firm's work as its own (outline-charter-2024 §8).

The studio also turns down "agency of record" enterprise retainers where the relationship would dilute below attention (outline-charter-2024 §1) and caps headcount at thirty to discipline against the bloat that pulls every successful studio toward agency-of-record gravity (outline-charter-2024 §20).
