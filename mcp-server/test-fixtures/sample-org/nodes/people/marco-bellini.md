# Marco Bellini

Strategy lead. Marco runs every strategy engagement end-to-end: scoping at pitch (with [client services](../units/client-services.md)), facilitating the narrative workshop on-site with the client (typically two half-days), writing the positioning document, running audience research with one of the strategists, and handing a written brief to the [design](../units/design.md) lead. He also does the competitive-audit work for new pitches when the studio is in front of a prospect (outline-roles-2025).

The strategy area carries four mid-level strategists who report to Marco. They run audience research interviews, transcribe and synthesise, write competitive audits, and develop naming systems on briefed projects (outline-roles-2025).
