# Iris Wynn

Founder and creative director. Iris started Outline & Co. in 2019 and shaped its current operating model in the 2024 charter. She runs the studio: reviews any deliverable that combines two or more disciplines, runs the quarterly studio finance review, sits at every enterprise-client kickoff, and personally leads the strategy + identity + product flow for the largest engagement of any given quarter (outline-roles-2025, outline-charter-2024 §9).

Iris does not own a client engagement portfolio of her own. She does not run weekly check-ins, and she does not produce deliverables on engagements that belong to a discipline lead (outline-roles-2025).
