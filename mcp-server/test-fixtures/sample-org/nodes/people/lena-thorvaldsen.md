# Lena Thorvaldsen

Design lead. Lena sits at every kickoff workshop, defines the visual language for the brief from [Marco Bellini](marco-bellini.md), supervises identity-system construction across the seven-person area, and reviews every deliverable before it leaves the studio per the senior-eyes-on-every-output rule (outline-charter-2024 §9, outline-roles-2025).

She also builds the brand book herself on every engagement. The brand book is the artefact the studio is best known for and the design lead does not delegate it (outline-roles-2025).

The design area under Lena currently has three senior designers, two mid-level designers, and one junior designer in a structured development arc paired with one of the seniors (outline-roles-2025).
