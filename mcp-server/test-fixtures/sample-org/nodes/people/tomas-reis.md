# Tomás Reis

Client services lead. Tomás runs the kickoff workshop for every engagement (scoping, calendar, weekly cadence agreement, single point of contact on the client side), runs the weekly check-in calls, manages the asset handover ritual at the end of each engagement, and runs scoping calls for new pitches alongside the relevant discipline lead (outline-roles-2025).

The two account managers under Tomás run weekly check-ins on three assigned engagements each, manage asset handover logistics, and chase client-side approvals. Tomás himself sits at every kickoff and every pitch — those moments are not delegated (outline-roles-2025).
