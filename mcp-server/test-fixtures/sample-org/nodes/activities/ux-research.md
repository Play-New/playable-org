The product lead runs UX research when the engagement asks for it: embedded with the client's product team, usability testing, observation, synthesis into a research report (outline-roles-2025).

User insights from research feed back into [UI design](ui-design.md) and into the [positioning](brand-positioning.md) brief on engagements where the strategy work hasn't yet closed.
