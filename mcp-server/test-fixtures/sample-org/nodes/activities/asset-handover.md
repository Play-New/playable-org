# Asset handover

The client services lead manages the asset-handover ritual at the end of each engagement. The handover package collects the brand book, identity system files, design-system documentation, UI screens, and any source files the client owns under the contract (outline-roles-2025).

The relevant discipline lead is present at handover; the lead and Tomás together walk the client through what they're receiving and confirm sign-off (outline-roles-2025).
