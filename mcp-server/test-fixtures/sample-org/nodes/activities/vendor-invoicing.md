# Vendor invoicing

Operators run the vendor invoice cycle every two weeks. The cadence is what lets the studio honour its commitment to pay vendor partners within 30 days of invoice receipt regardless of whether the underlying client has paid (outline-roles-2025, outline-charter-2024 §18).

Total vendor partner spend in 2025 was €0.6M; web development partners (€0.3M) and illustrators/photographers (€0.15M) are the largest line items (outline-annual-review-2025).
