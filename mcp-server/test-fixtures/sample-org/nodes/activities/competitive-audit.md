# Competitive audit

Strategists write competitive audits on briefed projects: who else exists in the category, what they sound like, where the white space is. The audit feeds the positioning brief (outline-roles-2025).

The strategy lead also does competitive-audit work for new pitches when the studio is in front of a prospect — the audit is the pitch's evidence base (outline-roles-2025).
