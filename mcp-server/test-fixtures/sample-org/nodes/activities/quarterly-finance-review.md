# Quarterly finance review

The founder runs the quarterly studio finance review. Operations prepares the materials (P&L, vendor spend, engagement margin, runway). The four discipline leads attend; decisions about price changes, vendor partner relationships, and discretionary spend are made in this meeting (outline-roles-2025).

This is distinct from the monthly studio review meeting where direction decisions are made by consensus of leads + founder (outline-roles-2025, outline-charter-2024 §14).
