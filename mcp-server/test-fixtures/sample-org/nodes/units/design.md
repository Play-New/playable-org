# Design

The design area runs every brand-identity engagement end-to-end (outline-charter-2024 §2). Identity systems, visual languages, brand books, motion systems — the area builds on top of the brief written by [strategy](strategy.md).

The area carries seven people: the lead, three senior designers, two mid-level designers, and one junior designer (outline-roles-2025). The design lead sits at every kickoff, defines the visual language for the brief, supervises identity-system construction, and reviews every deliverable before it leaves the studio. The lead also builds the brand book — the artefact the studio is best known for — and does not delegate it. Senior designers own one engagement at a time and carry the identity system build (typography, colour, grid, motion logic, illustration direction, photo direction). Mid-level designers support the senior on system build and own brand-book chapters under the lead's review (outline-roles-2025).

## Junior development

The junior designer is on a structured development arc: paired with a named senior, paid for studio learning time, and currently producing studio internal work (the studio website redesign, internal templates) plus shadowing client work. This is the practice of "junior development is structural, not opportunistic" codified in the charter (outline-charter-2024 §10).
