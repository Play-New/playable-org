# Operations

Operations is a thin function. Studio finance (P&L, vendor invoicing, client invoicing), hiring (job specs, candidate funnels, structured interviews), light HR (employment contracts, quarterly reviews), and vendor management (maintaining the studio's vendor partner list and the SLA conversations). Operations does not produce client work; it keeps the studio running (outline-charter-2024 §5, outline-roles-2025).

The area carries three people: the lead and two operators. Operators run financial reconciliation, run the vendor invoice cycle every two weeks (the cadence that lets the studio honour its 30-day-payment commitment to vendor partners), maintain the hiring pipeline funnel, and prepare materials for the quarterly studio finance review the founder runs (outline-roles-2025, outline-charter-2024 §18).

## Governance role

Operations runs the agenda and notes of the monthly studio review meeting where the four discipline leads, the operations lead, and the founder make decisions about new disciplines, new client tiers, headcount changes, and major vendor partner relationships. Operations does not have a vote on those decisions — operations runs the room (outline-roles-2025, outline-charter-2024 §14).
