# Strategic partners

Other studios and research firms Outline knows and trusts. When a client need exceeds the studio's capacity or scope, Outline says so and recommends the studio in its network that fits — the studio does not subcontract under its own name (outline-charter-2024 §8).

The relationship is informal: mutual referral, no joint-venture or revenue share. Partners include a small set of strategy-only firms, a couple of digital-product engineering shops, and two type foundries the studio has long license relationships with.
