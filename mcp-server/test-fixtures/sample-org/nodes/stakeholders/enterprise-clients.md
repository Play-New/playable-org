# Enterprise clients

A small number of enterprise relationships, typically one or two per year by design. The work is the same as mid-market but the cadence is longer and the pricing is higher; one or two studio people are effectively co-located with the client team for the duration (outline-charter-2024 §16).

In 2025 the studio ran two enterprise engagements totalling €1.5M (33% of revenue): a €600k 9-month embedded engagement with a fintech client (closed clean, no follow-on) and a €900k brand + product + design-system engagement with an industrial-machinery client (now in a 12-month follow-on retainer with embedded product designer presence two days per week) (outline-annual-review-2025).
