# Outline & Co. — Studio charter

_Adopted by the founding partners, January 2024. This document supersedes the
informal practices of the previous five years and codifies what the studio
commits to doing, not doing, and how it will operate._

## What the studio is for

Outline & Co. is an independent creative studio that helps mid-market companies
articulate what they sell, design how it shows up, and ship the digital
products that carry it forward. The studio works as a single team across three
disciplines — **strategy**, **design**, and **digital product** — held
together by tight client services and a small operations function.

The studio commits to taking on engagements where the brand and the product
are co-developed, not where one is asked to dress the other after the fact. We
turn down purely tactical work (campaign creative, ad-set production,
single-deliverable logos detached from a system) and we turn down enterprise
"agency of record" retainers where the relationship would dilute below
attention.

## What we do

§1. **Brand strategy.** Positioning, audience research, naming systems,
competitive audits, narrative workshops. The strategy lead runs these and
hands a written brief to design.

§2. **Brand identity.** Identity systems, visual languages, brand books,
motion systems. The design lead runs these on top of the strategy brief.

§3. **Digital product.** UI design for B2B web and apps; design systems for
clients who maintain their own product engineering; UX research embedded with
the client's product team. The product lead runs these.

§4. **Client services across the three.** The client services lead runs
kickoff workshops, weekly check-ins, asset handovers, and scoping for the
next pitch.

§5. **Operations as a thin function.** Studio finance, hiring, vendor
management, light HR. Operations does not produce client work; it keeps the
studio running.

## What we don't do

§6. We do not run paid-media campaigns. The studio does not buy or place
advertising; clients work with media agencies for that.

§7. We do not produce logos as one-off deliverables. A logo without an
identity system around it goes stale within twelve months and we refuse to
ship work we know will go stale.

§8. We do not subcontract design or strategy work to other agencies under our
name. If a client need exceeds our capacity we say so and we recommend the
studio in our network that fits.

## Studio principles (governance)

§9. **Senior eyes on every output.** No client deliverable leaves the studio
without the lead of its discipline reviewing it. There are three leads:
strategy, design, product. The creative director (founder) reviews any
deliverable that combines two or more disciplines.

§10. **Junior development is structural, not opportunistic.** Every junior
designer or strategist has a named senior counterpart and a quarterly
development plan with the operations lead. Time spent on development is
billable to the studio, not to clients.

§11. **Vendor partners over employees for production-only work.** Web
development, video editing, illustration, photography, type-foundry licensing
— these stay vendor relationships. The studio doesn't try to be a production
shop.

§12. **Prices are clear and posted.** Engagement scopes carry a fixed price
disclosed at pitch. The studio does not bill hourly; the studio bills
deliverables. Disputes about price changes go to the founder.

§13. **The studio commits to its employees on development, not on tenure.**
Junior employees can expect a structured development arc; the studio does not
guarantee permanent employment if the work doesn't fit.

§14. **Decisions about studio direction are made by the three leads + the
founder.** New disciplines, new client tiers, new offices — these need
consensus across the four. Hiring within an existing discipline is the lead's
call.

## Cardinal commitments

§15. **To mid-market clients.** Outline & Co. delivers the strategy →
identity → digital product flow for mid-market companies, on a fixed-price
engagement scope agreed at pitch. Standard engagement: 12–16 weeks. Standard
deliverables: positioning document, identity system, digital product
artefact (website, app, or design system).

§16. **To enterprise clients.** A small number of enterprise relationships,
typically 1–2 per year, run as 6–9 month engagements with embedded studio
presence (one or two studio people effectively co-located with the client
team). The work is the same as mid-market but the cadence is longer and the
pricing is higher.

§17. **To employees on development.** Junior staff are guaranteed a
structured development arc with a named senior, quarterly review, and
training time accounted for in the studio's allocation.

§18. **To vendor partners on payment.** The studio pays vendor invoices
within 30 days of receipt, regardless of whether the underlying client has
paid. This is what allows us to work with the small studios and freelance
specialists we want to work with.

## Studio organisation

§19. The studio operates as five **areas**: strategy, design, digital
product, client services, operations. There are no formal divisions above
the areas; everything reports to the founder as creative director. Each
discipline area has a lead; client services and operations each have their
own lead.

§20. Headcount is capped at thirty. Past that, the studio either splits or
turns work away. The cap is a discipline against the agency-of-record
gravity that pulls every successful studio toward bloat.
