# Outline & Co. — Annual review, 2025

_Maintained by the operations lead with the founder. Issued each January
covering the prior calendar year. Numbers are studio internal._

## Headcount

End-of-year headcount: 23 people. Up from 19 at end of 2024.

Distribution:
- Strategy: 5
- Design: 7
- Digital product: 5
- Client services: 3
- Operations: 3

The studio is six people short of the 30-person cap declared in the charter.

## Revenue

2025 revenue: €4.6M. Up from €3.4M in 2024.

By engagement type:
- Mid-market engagements: €2.8M (61% of revenue) across 14 engagements
  averaging €200k each.
- Enterprise engagements: €1.5M (33% of revenue) across two engagements,
  one at €600k (a 9-month embedded engagement with a fintech client) and
  one at €900k (a brand + product + design-system engagement with an
  industrial-machinery client).
- Studio-internal investment: €0.3M (6% of revenue), unallocated to
  client work — this is the time spent on the studio website redesign,
  internal templates, the junior designer's development arc, and the
  three pitches that didn't convert.

## Operating costs by area

- Strategy: €0.6M (mostly salaries, no other significant costs)
- Design: €0.9M (salaries plus the type-foundry license bench)
- Digital product: €0.7M (salaries plus the prototyping-tool subscriptions)
- Client services: €0.4M (salaries)
- Operations: €0.4M (salaries plus accounting, legal, office)
- Studio overhead: €0.5M (lease, utilities, equipment, vendor partner bench
  retainers)

Total cost: €3.5M. Operating margin: €1.1M (24%).

## Vendor partner spend

The studio paid €0.6M to its vendor partner network in 2025: web
development partners (€0.3M), illustrators and photographers (€0.15M),
type-foundry licensing (€0.05M), printers (€0.07M), other (€0.03M). All
invoices were paid within 30 days per the studio's commitment.

## Pitches and conversion

The studio entered 11 pitches in 2025. Won 8 (73% conversion). The three
that didn't convert: one mid-market client where the timeline was wrong
for the studio (project demanded 6-week turnaround the studio couldn't
honour without breaking the senior-eyes-on-every-output rule), one
enterprise client that wanted media buying included (the studio doesn't
do that), and one mid-market client where the brand and the product
engineering were already locked at separate vendors and the studio
declined to "dress" them.

## Notable client outcomes

The largest engagement of 2025 was the industrial-machinery enterprise
project. Output: positioning document, identity system, marketing site,
internal product UI, and a design system the client now maintains. The
engagement is the studio's first one with a 12-month follow-on arrangement
where Outline maintains a small embedded presence (one product designer,
two days per week) on a retainer basis. This arrangement is consistent
with the charter's enterprise-engagement clause but is a new pattern the
operations lead is watching for scope creep.

The fintech enterprise engagement closed clean at month nine, no
follow-on. Brand book and design system handed off, client product team
took over.

Of the fourteen mid-market engagements, eleven closed on time and on
fixed price; three required scope-renegotiation conversations (handled
by the founder per §12 of the charter).

## Junior development

The studio currently has one junior designer on a structured development
arc; she joined in March 2025. Her senior counterpart is one of the three
senior designers; her quarterly reviews are run by the operations lead
with the design lead present. Through 2025 she has shipped two studio
internal artefacts (the studio website redesign, the internal proposal
template) and is now shadowing her second client engagement.

The studio plans to bring on one junior strategist and one junior product
designer in 2026, holding the cap by ageing the studio profile rather
than adding senior capacity.
