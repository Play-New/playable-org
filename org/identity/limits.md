# Limits

REPLACE ME. The limits node lists what the organization has chosen not to do. Two categories: legal limits (boundaries imposed by the charter, statute, regulator) and operational limits (boundaries the organization itself has chosen).

Each item is cited. The point of this file is not to enumerate everything the organization doesn't do (an infinite list), but to document the few cases where the negative choice itself is meaningful — where someone could reasonably expect the organization to do X but it explicitly does not.

Example shape:

## Statutory limits

- The organization does not [activity X], per `(charter §Y)`.
- Activity Z is excluded from the operating perimeter, per `(charter art.W)`.

## Operational limits

- The organization has chosen not to operate in [domain N], because `(decision-doc-id)`.
