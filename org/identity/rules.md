# Rules

REPLACE ME. The rules node documents the formal governance structure: legal form (limited company, foundation, association, public body), the governance bodies (board, councils, committees, statutory auditors), the decision rules (who can decide what, with which majorities, against which constraints).

Anchored on the founding documents (statute, articles, governance charter) and possibly on internal policies (compliance models, risk management frameworks, codes of conduct).

Example shape:

## Legal form

The organization is a [type], registered in [register/jurisdiction] since [date], per `(charter art.X)`.

## Governance bodies

- [Body 1]: [N] members, [appointment rule], [scope of decisions].
- [Body 2]: ...

## Decision rules

- Decisions over [type] require [majority] of [body].
- Conflict of interest rules: ...
