# Mission

REPLACE ME. The mission is the cited statement of why this organization exists. It is short, observable, and attributed to a primary source (statute, founding charter, articles of association). It is not aspirational copy.

A good mission node:
- Quotes one sentence verbatim from the founding source.
- Explains what the organization does in plain language.
- Cites the source with `(<source-id>)`.

Example shape (delete and replace with your own):

> Quote from the statute or charter, ≤3 lines.

Paragraph in plain language explaining what the organization actually does, citing the same source `(your-source-id §X)`.
